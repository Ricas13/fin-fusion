# Prepared monetary transfer (initial execution path)

Default off. Do not set PAID_VALUE_TRANSFER_ENABLED=true until all web and webhook workers run this migration and end-to-end settlement, reversal and rolling deployment tests pass.

Supported candidate: one active verified cash or fully evidenced mixed cash/earned-credit one-off purchase (Stripe, PayPal or Plisio), same service lane, changing access to a new Stripe recurring agreement at a verified normal base or access-variant price. No multiple overlapping sources, unsupported currencies, recurring-to-recurring replacement, or operator grants. Existing identical-access carry is unchanged. A new verified first Stripe period is required. A fully discounted or fully credit-funded first invoice is supported only after a completed zero-amount Checkout, with a full discount, zero invoice balance, and saved payment method for subsequent renewals.

Preparation checks source and destination before checkout; settlement revalidates live payment and actual destination first billing term, freezes exact rational monetary value and FX evidence in the carry ledger, replaces old entitlement and grants the converted days in the same transaction. Existing reversal/return paths use the same carry ledger. Unexpected evidence fails closed. No new refunds or provider mutations occur. Round up once to whole service days; no monetary rounding before conversion.

This is a scoped initial implementation, not a claim of full upgrade/downgrade support. Review database and provider-specific regression coverage before enabling.

Same-access purchases with a different recorded currency must also use monetary valuation rather than copying days one-for-one. A completed transfer acknowledges replays without resurrecting reversed value. The receiving Stripe checkout and first invoice must both attest the same positive amount and currency; mixed receiving Stripe checkouts can also settle their exact earned-credit reservation atomically with the paid transfer. The existing positive provider minimum applies; a missing or underfunded reservation rolls back all local entitlement changes. For a mixed-funded source, its redeemed credit and provider-paid cash remain separate exact fractions in both currencies. The existing source-payment-loss policy reverses that purchase's transferred service; no provider cash refund can include earned credit, and the transfer creates no new credit or debit. Pure-credit purchases still require a separate execution path.

New full service-credit redemptions freeze their original commercial terms and can be valued as a source for supported Stripe transfers using their exact redemption debit and complete funding allocations. No provider cash is attributed to that source. Historical redemptions without frozen terms, are not automatically transferred.
