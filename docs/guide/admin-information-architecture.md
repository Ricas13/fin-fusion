# Administration information architecture

CAPTAiNFiN administration follows canonical ownership rather than duplicating controls across pages.

- **People**: Customers and Jellyfin import/claim workflows.
- **Servers**: Jellyfin servers, libraries, users/import, playback operations, fleet operations and Stremio sources.
- **Plans**: Direct customer plans, Stremio/bundles, access rules and storefront order.
- **Commerce / Payment Providers**: revenue visibility, billing, provider mappings and payment-risk policy.
- **Automation**: scheduled jobs, provisioning work and operational recovery workflows.
- **Settings**: platform-wide identity, integrations, notifications, security and support policy.
- **My account**: the signed-in administrator's own profile, notification preferences and security.

Customer-specific support belongs inside Customer 360. Invitation onboarding is retired and must not return as a navigation destination.
