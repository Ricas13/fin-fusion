# Account, passwords and security

This page explains the credentials a customer may encounter in CAPTAiNFiN.

## Portal password

Your CAPTAiNFiN portal password is used to sign in to the customer account area. It is not automatically reused as your Jellyfin password.

## Jellyfin password

If your subscription includes normal Jellyfin access, you may be asked to set a separate Jellyfin password. Changing the Jellyfin password does not change the CAPTAiNFiN portal password.

## Password reset

Email password reset changes the **portal password only**. If transactional email is unavailable, browser-based password reset may be temporarily unavailable and support may be required.

## Two-factor authentication

Where customer 2FA is available, enabling it adds a second sign-in factor to the portal account. Keep recovery codes somewhere safe and private.

## Email address changes

Security-sensitive email changes may use a staged verification flow. Until the new address is successfully verified, the existing verified address can remain the sign-in/reset identity.

## Stremio credentials

When Stremio service becomes available, its addon installation credential will be separate from both your portal and Jellyfin passwords. Rotating that credential invalidates the previous addon installation credential.

## Need help?

When contacting support, include your portal username and a description of the problem. Do **not** send passwords, 2FA recovery codes, Jellyfin API keys or payment-provider secrets.
